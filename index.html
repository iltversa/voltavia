Straight answers

The things people ask before they buy

Do I need a licence or registration?

Our 25 km/h models are built to sit in the low speed category, which in most states means no licence and no registration. Rules change by state, so we confirm your exact case in writing before you pay anything.

Is 90 km real, or a lab number?

It is the rated figure for the 60V 32Ah pack. What you get depends on load, tyre pressure, terrain and how much stop and start you ride. A loaded rider on a hilly route should plan for less. Ask us and we will give you the honest number for your commute.

What happens to the battery after a few years?

Lithium packs lose capacity gradually rather than failing overnight. The pack is removable and replaceable, so a tired battery is a part you swap, not a scooter you scrap.

Can I charge it in my flat?

Yes. The pack lifts out and the charger runs off a normal wall socket. That is the whole reason it is removable.

Where do I get it serviced?

Call us and we will point you at the nearest service partner. An electric scooter has far fewer wearing parts than a petrol one, so most visits are brakes, tyres and a battery health check.
